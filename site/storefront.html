Before your first bite.

A few things you might be wondering.

How will I place an order?

When ordering opens, choose your treats, add them to your box, and send your order request by message. Availability, pickup, and payment will be confirmed directly. For now, you can explore the sample menu and try the bag.

Where can I pick up my order?

Pickup location, available days, and preparation times are being finalized. The confirmed details will be added here before ordering opens.

What about allergies and ingredients?

These sample bakes may contain wheat, milk, eggs, soy, and nuts. Final ingredient and allergen information will be confirmed for each product. Please check directly before ordering if you have an allergy; no allergen-free options are currently promised.

Can I order for a gift or a special occasion?

Yes, you can prepare an enquiry for a gift, gathering, or special batch using “Plan something sweet.” Final options, quantities, pricing, and availability will be confirmed when the shop opens.
