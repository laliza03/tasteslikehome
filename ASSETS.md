# TastesLikeHome image assets

All six photographs were made with the built-in Image Generation tool. They are illustrative mock photography, not photos of actual products. Only original generated photographs are included in the website; the supplied reference images and their watermarks are not published.

Assets are saved in `dist/assets/`. Original generated PNGs were converted to WebP without changing their composition. Reference colours: chocolate #3B2418, cream #F2E8DA, baby blue #8EC5FF.
